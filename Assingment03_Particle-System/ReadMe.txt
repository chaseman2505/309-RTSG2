Camera Controls

- Press C to switch between Focus and First-Person (FP) camera modes.

Focus Mode

- Hold Alt and drag with the left mouse button to rotate the camera.

- Hold Alt and drag with the right mouse button to zoom.

- Hold Alt and drag with the middle mouse button to pan.

First-Person (FP) Mode

- Use W, A, S, and D to move the camera.

- Hold and drag the middle mouse button to rotate the camera.
